The emotion balloons in client/emotes.js (BALLOONS: the heart, !, ?, the anger vein, the sweat drop, HAHA, Zzz, the light
bulb and the typing dots) are our own 16x16 pixel drawings in the game's palette, made after the pixel style of the
"Emotes Pack" by Kenney (www.kenney.nl, https://opengameart.org/content/emotes-pack), released under Creative Commons
Zero (CC0 1.0, http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): free to use in personal, educational and commercial
projects. Credit is not required; we give it gladly. No file of the pack is copied into the game.
