# The concept checklist (pass 5, 2026-11-19)

**608 items: 536 in the game, 53 missing, 19 too small to show.** (11-20: 24 of the missing ones built - the first batch of "the next pass's work" at the end; pass 5 left it at 512 in, 77 missing.)

Every outfit, piece and creator option on the concept sheets (`docs/characters/concepts/`, 94 images), against the
wardrobe (`shared/lookcore.js`) and the creator (`client/creator.js`). An item is a thing a player would look for: one
line can name a whole outfit, and a piece many sheets show is listed where it matters (a few on more than one sheet).

- **in** - you can wear it today; the column names the piece (the game's own name). "(pass on)" means only the
  character pass draws it or draws it as itself; with the pass off it is hidden or drawn as its base. A stand-in is
  named as such ("Trench coat" for a duster).
- **missing** - nothing in the wardrobe does it; the note says what is nearest.
- **too small** - cannot be shown on a figure 25 art pixels tall.

Any piece takes any of the 40 cloth colours and a trim colour; tops, jackets, bottoms and sets also take a pattern
(stripes, check, camo, floral, tie-dye). So a colourway on a sheet is "in" when the piece is.

**How it was made.** From the catalogue (`CONCEPTS.md`, written with every image open, one by one) and pass 2's table
in `NOTES.md`, checked name by name against the wardrobe's list; in this pass I reopened only the hair sheets (CB3-F,
CB3-M, CC2-A) and one creator mock-up (CC1-A). The other sheets were not looked at again - a small piece the
catalogue did not note is not here.
