## A2. How long to join the police
25 Samaritan points and no felonies on record (`ENFORCER_MIN_SAMARITAN`). The points come from: a lost wallet back +8,
someone collapsed helped up +6, a fight broken up +4, a stolen purse back +10, a revive +5, every legit delivery +2,
a crime reported +2, the deep-sea charter +2, a lost pet home. A happening is set up near a player every ~150 s
(`HAPPEN_EVERY_S`). **Est. 10-20 minutes** for a player who answers the city's little events, ~13 deliveries otherwise.
Bounty hunting needs 10. Reading: quick, maybe too quick for the badge to feel earned - but it's the honest door in.

## A3. How hard crime is punished; getting Samaritan back
- **Samaritan points never go down for a crime** - nothing in the code subtracts them. Crime adds Criminal XP (half the
  crime's heat), seen or not.
- What blocks the badge is a **felony on record**, and only a crime someone saw and called in goes on it. It can be paid
  off at a courthouse or Police HQ: $750 a felony (`FELONY_FINE`). A record of 3+ stars also locks duty for a while
  (`careers.dutyLock`).
- Busted: $250 a star (`BUST_FINE_PER_STAR`), illegal guns confiscated, legal ones to the stash; a cell, prison only at 5
  stars with a long record. Knocked down: cash and carried items dropped (guns as pickups for 60 s), $150 at a hospital
  or $200 for the ambulance.
- **Changed 11-22** (his note "too easily"): the first star now takes 36 heat on the no-star tally (was 24); small
  crimes 5 sightings (was 4); running someone down is a lesser crime, not a felony (22 heat, was 40 and a felony). So an
  honest player's accident - a pedestrian clipped, a bumped car - is a warning, not a record.

## A4. Earning vs losing: does crime pay, do honest jobs pay
| Crime | Pay | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| Rob a till | ~$25 a wad every 0.8 s, a till $300-$2,000 by kind ($5,000 a bank's) | a silent alarm at 10-20 s, 1 star and growing; the cash is hot money (a pawn shop's 40% cut to clean it) |
| A carbon-gold crate (armored truck, every 30 min, 2 crates) | up to $8,000 at a fence | shown on everyone's map; the guards; others ambush you |
| Steel / iron crates (the ship, hourly) | $1,200 / $3,200 | carried in the open |
| Heist contract (Captain rank) | $6,000+ plus the vault | every unit responds |
| The mail train strongbox | $3,000 fenced | felony |
| Gang war (3x a day) | $120 a takedown, $1,500 a win | - |

**Reading:** crime's big money is in the scheduled events (crates, heists), each worth several hours of honest work in
ten minutes - if you get away with it. Small crime (tills) pays about like honest work, with the heat. That's the
shape the progression plan wanted ("big money from visible crate runs"). What's lost on a death is the cash you carry
and your guns, never the bank, the homes or the cars: **keep money in the bank** is the lesson, and the Guide says the
first half of it only. No infinite-riches risk found in the rules alone; the sinks are upkeep, ammo, cosmetics, vests,
insurance, the crew's upkeep.

## A6. The illegal mushrooms
Ghostglass Caps grow at old stumps, root plates and clearings in the redwoods (glowing at night). Picking them already
says so: *"Illegal. Only the Back-Alley Exchange buys them - get caught with them and the police take them."* (the
pick's line, in warning colors). The Exchange pays $70 each. Missing: the price in that line, anything that points a
player to them before they stumble on one, and a Guide line. Proposal: the pick's line gains the price, the Guide gets
one entry from the data, and the crime career's What's next offers "Ghostglass grows in the redwoods at night" once.

## A7. Crime nobody caught
Already the rule: a crime nobody saw, or nobody called in, costs nothing but Criminal XP - no heat, no record, no
Samaritan loss (`law.js crime`: no witnesses -> "nobody saw it"). So a player can mix crime and honest work and keep the
badge's road open, as long as they're not seen. **Gap:** the Guide doesn't say so. Proposal: one Guide entry, "Clean
record, quiet money", explaining exactly that and the risk.
