# Fishing concepts FC01-FC22: the catalogue

The owner's fishing concepts of 2026-10-18 (07:10, twenty and then two more), filed as `FCnn.jpg`. They are for the
fishing system to come (task #277, Stardew-style fishing: charge to cast, the timing game, the loot) and what goes
round it: the species and where they live, the legendary catches, the fishing places, the gear, the tournaments, night
squid fishing, crab pots, and what you do with a catch.

Each entry gives what it shows, then what it feeds in the fishing system.

## The legendary catches
- **FC01**: five legendary catches, each hooked in its own place, each fish huge and lit:
  - a golden koi in a lily pond under cherry blossom, fished from a jetty;
  - a sturgeon over a sunken drop-off, hooked from a skiff;
  - a rainbow trout in the white water under a waterfall (a rainbow in the spray);
  - a giant catfish in a brown pool under a stone arch bridge;
  - a glowing white ghost koi in a dark cave pool, fished from a jetty by lantern light.
  - **Feeds:** one legendary fish per kind of water, its own spot, the glow on the rarest.
- **FC02**: five more legendary catches:
  - an alligator gar in a mangrove swamp, fished from a jon boat;
  - a huge halibut over the sand among a pier's pilings;
  - a tarpon leaping by the mangroves at sunset, hooked from a flats boat;
  - a blue marlin hooked behind a sport-fishing boat;
  - a giant squid at night under a trawler's lights.
  - **Feeds:** the salt-water and night legendaries; the marlin and the squid need a boat.
