# The beta walkthrough (pre-beta list G)

The checklist the owner plays through on desktop and phone before the beta - also as a Sheet to tick: https://claude.ai/artifact/6nvwNiRbn6kBRERfJF7JKD (made 11-27 night). Every Problem becomes an item in `docs/audit/PREBETA-LIST.md`. Keep it in step with the game: a new system gets a row (and its Guide entry).

| # | Area | Try this | It should |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Start | Open the game fresh on the phone (a new character) | Title, the age line and consent once; the arrival by train at the station you pick; one question, a first goal of at most 3 steps; skippable |
| 2 | Start | Earn the first car the way your answer says (buy at the used lot, or steal in the alley) | The used lot / alley a short walk from your station; the car is yours after; no free starter car for new players |
| 3 | Start | Phone upright, then on its side | Upright: layout A (arc round the aim stick, chat and emotes over the move stick, one MENU top right); sideways: as before |
| 4 | On foot | Walk, run (push the stick all the way), sprint, dive | Smooth; sprint drains stamina; dive rolls you out of the way |
| 5 | On foot | Walk into a shop's door | You stay drawn at the door until the inside shows; no hop over the front; the way-out hint if the inside is slow |
| 6 | On foot | Stand under an awning, behind a tall building | The building goes see-through round you |
| 7 | Driving | Get into any parked car, drive, brake, reverse | Taking one that isn't yours is a crime only if seen; handling feels right; engine sound |
| 8 | Driving | Drive fast through a fence, bench or bus shelter | It goes flat; anyone sitting is thrown |
| 9 | Driving | Drive into shallow, then deeper water | Splash, then slower, then the engine floods and stalls (8 s; 30 in an off-road car) |
| 10 | Driving | Bump a slow car once, then again | The driver stops, honks, shouts ("Hey! Watch it!"); a second bump: a tough one gets out to have it out, most drive off |
| 11 | Driving | Have my car delivered (phone) when far from it | A valet parks it a short walk away, about 12 s later; it shows on the map |
| 12 | Traffic | Watch a busy four-way for a few minutes (lights and no lights) | Traffic keeps flowing; no car stuck in the box more than ~30 s |
| 13 | Transit | Bus: wait at a stop, board with ACT, get off at another stop | Free; the Transit app shows when the next comes |
| 14 | Transit | Train: board at a station, ride, get off | Free; the platform counts down to the next train |
| 15 | Transit | Taxi: set a waypoint, call one from the Transit app | It drives to your waypoint |
| 16 | Transit | Ferry: walk aboard; on a car ferry drive up to the stern | Free; you ride across |
| 17 | Boats | Hire a kayak ($30) and paddle | Seated, a double paddle; it surges each stroke and glides; turns slowly; splashes, no engine |
| 18 | Boats | Hire a jet ski or speedboat, drive, return | 5 minutes' hire; handles on the water |
| 19 | Skate | Take a board (skate park rack or buy one), ride, roll onto grass | On grass/dirt/sand you step off with it in your hands; hop back on on pavement; a board left behind waits |
| 20 | Aircraft | Fly a helicopter / plane if you have one (or the dev menu) | Takes off, lands; CLIMB / DOWN on touch |
| 21 | Combat | Punch, block, use a melee weapon, a gun (aim and fire) | Hits land; HIT shows what you hit with; reload; the aim stick fires when pushed into its ring (touch) |
| 22 | Combat | Shove a passer-by; point a gun at one; fire near them | Most back off with a word, hands up; worse and they run, hide behind a car or call the police; armed ones may draw |
| 23 | Combat | Fight an armed NPC who has a car to hide behind | They duck behind it and pop up to fire |
| 24 | Bikers | Hit a biker at the Rusty Spur | The one you hit and the nearest come at you; the rest come out shouting and join two at a time |
| 25 | Bikers | Then get away on wheels | The club mounts up and chases you |
| 26 | Police | Commit a seen crime; get 1-2 stars; run | Stars add with heat; at low stars the police are easier and you can outrun them on foot |
| 27 | Police | Get to 4-5 stars | Harder pursuit; SWAT draw fast and shoot tight; cops use cover |
| 28 | Police | Get busted; mash FIRE to break free | A few seconds to fight it at low stars |
| 29 | Police | Spray & Go: drive in unseen | A new color and a clean wanted level ($150) |
| 30 | Police | Join the force (25+ Samaritan points, no felonies) at a station | Uniform, cruiser, patrol calls |
| 31 | Knocked down | Get knocked out | Cash and carried things drop; the knocked-out screen: call help, ambulance, pick where to wake |
| 32 | Jobs | Open the phone's Jobs app; take a job; quit it from the job card | One job at a time; pay shown; Quit job at the top |
| 33 | Jobs | Office work at the Employment Center (by Park East Tower) | A round of 10 quick tasks in 45 s, $2 each, $4 more for a clean round |
| 34 | Jobs | The job computer at the Employment Center | The work in town that suits you, each a waypoint (no ad button while ads are off) |
| 35 | Jobs | Mail carrier, deliveries, taxi/bus driving | Each pays as its card says; the work vehicle returned for $150 more |
| 36 | Jobs | Paramedic (sign on at a hospital) | You wear the paramedic's uniform; the ambulance can drive off forward; your clothes back when you quit |
| 37 | Jobs | Forklift at a warehouse: 6 pallets | Lift with ACT, set in the lit slot; pay per pallet |
| 38 | Jobs | Farm: pick a ripe field; a ranger task | 3 picks fill a crate ($45 at the farm stand) |
| 39 | Goals | Phone: What's next, daily goals | Three ideas each with one button; three goals a day, $300 more for all three |
| 40 | Crime | Hold up a store (gun at the clerk); bank the hot money 75 m away | The till handed over; a silent alarm 10-20 s in; hot money in a bag on your back |
| 41 | Crime | Steal a car and sell / store it; the black market | Works as the Guide says |
| 42 | Shops | Buy something at a counter (cash first, then bank) | It goes into your Inventory |
| 43 | Shops | Clothes shop: try and buy clothes; the style counter | Your look updates; name colours/plates buyable |
| 44 | Character | Title screen: CHARACTER - change body, face, hair, clothes; save to My looks | The My looks strip under the preview; tap one to wear it |
| 45 | Inventory | Open the Inventory (first time after joining) | Shows at once (no empty frame); on a phone every button big enough to tap |
| 46 | Inventory | Wear / take off, drop, use a bandage; drag to the quick bar | Works on touch, mouse and pad |
| 47 | Inventory | Stash at home / gun shop; your car's trunk | Things move between them |
| 48 | Homes | Buy a home (Homes app); go in; wardrobe; garage | Blink in 2.5 s at the door; car in the garage |
| 49 | Homes | Mortgage, upkeep | 20% down; upkeep from the bank each day |
| 50 | Business | Own a business (farm, nightclub, auto shop) | A day's takings in its till each in-game day you're in town |
| 51 | Fishing | Take the rod out, face water, hold ACT to cast, reel | A bite, the fight's meter up the right side (prompt clear of it); fish in the cooler; sell at a bait shop |
| 52 | Activities | Pool at a bar ($1): play the computer | Eight-ball plays; (the Rusty Spur's table art is the art chat's) |
| 53 | Activities | Bowling, golf, chess, air hockey, arcade machines | Each starts, plays and pays as its Guide says; Quit from its card works |
| 54 | Activities | Tennis with the club pro; shoot hoops | Games run; passers-by keep off the court while you play |
| 55 | Activities | Casino floor: roulette, blackjack, slots | Plays end to end against the house |
| 56 | Activities | Car soccer at the Waves Arena | Lobby, NPC drivers fill teams, kick-off, goals |
| 57 | Activities | Races: a raceway / water race; the River Run (kayak) | Join at the start, gates, finish; the River Run is back |
| 58 | Activities | Cinema, the drive-in after dark | Tickets, a film on the screen |
| 59 | Activities | Caves and mines with a light | In with ACT; dark inside; light on; the way out shows |
| 60 | Activities | Street dice in a Southside alley | $20 a roll, even money |
| 61 | People | Walk Midtown, the Strip at night, a park, the beach | People walk, idle, dance (spaced, not in fountains), lean, busk; nobody on furniture |
| 62 | Animals | Watch deer or a herd in the woods, birds in town | Each moves on its own (no two in lockstep); they sense you and react |
| 63 | Phone | Open every phone app (Jobs, Transit, Skills, Guide, Messages, Camera, Homes, City feed, Report bug) | Each opens, reads right, closes; the Guide's entries match what the game does |
| 64 | Chat | Chat: a quick line, a typed line; emotes, dances, poses | Bubbles over heads; emotes play |
| 65 | Friends | Add a friend; a crew invite | Requests arrive; crew tag shows |
| 66 | Menu | MENU: map, phone, bag, people, settings, report a bug, log off | All reachable; upright one MENU button (with the phone's dot) |
| 67 | Settings | Graphics presets, sound, controls, safety mode, account | Each takes effect; Fullscreen where offered |
| 68 | Accounts | Sign in (Google / Discord / email), sign out, switch account; guest | Progress kept on link; names right |
| 69 | Map | Tap the minimap, set a waypoint, route | The map opens; a route along the roads |
| 70 | Night/weather | Play at night and in the rain | Lamps on; headlights; rain streaks catch the lights; no stutter |
| 71 | Performance | Drive a few minutes on the phone at night in rain | Smooth enough; note any hitches |
| 72 | Server | During a restart | "Server updating - back in a few minutes", reconnects by itself |
